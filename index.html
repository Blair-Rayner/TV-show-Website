SITCOMS AND COMEDY

FRIENDS

★★★★★

If you want a comfort show, this is it. I have rewatched this show so many times simply because it is so enjoyable. The show is ten seasons long but also easily bingeable. The episodes are only 20 minutes so they are easy to watch when you only have a little bit of time which is very convenient. 

THE BIG BANG THEORY

★★★★★

This is arguably the funniest show I have watched. The characters are extremely well written and the humor is easy going and witty. It is quite a long show, however it still feels like it is over too quickly. The writers did an incredible job wrapping up the show and it is one of the most satisfying endings I have watched. 

SUPERSTORE

★★★★☆

This show is so chaotic that you never know what is going to happen. The characters are likeable and the episodes are funny and ridiculous. Another very well rounded up show, with everyone resulting with their own happy ending. I would recommend this to anyone who likes a good comedy. 

YOUNG SHELDON

★★★★☆

This show is the sequel to The Big Bang Theory. It follows the main character Sheldon through his childhood years, filling in a lot of the blanks that are left unknown in the original. This show is homely and a realistic representation of a young family evolving over the years. If you have watched the big bang theory, I would highly recommend this show to you. 

GEORGIE AND MANDY'S FIRST MARRIAGE

★★★☆☆

A spinoff of Young Sheldon, This show follows Sheldon’s brother as he navigates his marriage as a young adult with a small child. If you are attached to the story line that emerges from the previous two shows, this show is a good watch with lots of funny scenes, however the episodes are short and the show itself isn’t that interesting. 

AMERICAN HOUSEWIFE

★★★☆☆

This show was enjoyable, however I didn’t find it very enticing. The final season was a let down and the show got a bit repetitive, however it did feature good humor and some really good episodes. They have a few cameo appearances throughout the show which was really cool and fun to watch. 

FULL HOUSE

★★★☆☆

This show is a classic family sitcom. It's heartwarming, funny, and has great characters that you can relate to. The show is a great watch for anyone who likes family-oriented comedy. It is quite childish and outdated and the camera work is not very good, however it is still a good show to watch if you are looking for a lighthearted comedy. 

ALEXA AND KATIE

★★☆☆☆

This show is a romantic comedy that follows the lives of two best friends as they navigate love, relationships, and career ambitions. It's a light-hearted show with plenty of humor and heart. However, some episodes can feel a bit predictable and the show has a childish feel that a younger person would prefer. 

POLICE AND MEDICAL DRAMA

THE ROOKIE

★★★★★

Thsi is my favourite show ever! I have watched it over seven times because of how addicting and entertaining it is. The plotlines are engaging and the characters are well-developed. There is plenty of suspense and intensity as well as good humor. If you havent watched it then you should start now! 

THE RESIDENT

★★★★★

This is my favourite medical show. If you enjoyed watching The Rookie, you will love this show. It features similar character depth and compelling storylines. With only six seasons, it is over much too soon. The camera work and editing is outstanding and all of the actors do a fenomanal job of bringing their roles to life. 

THE ROOKIE FEDS

★★★★☆

This is a spin off of The Rookie and follows some of the side characters from the original show. It has a similar tone and style, making it a great continuation for fans of the series. The caases are more dramatic and intruiging so if you like being on the edge of your seat you should watch this show! 

HOUSE

★★★☆☆

This show follows Dr. House who is a brilliant but arrogant diagnostician who can solve complex medical cases. The plot lines are extremely well written and thought about, however I found the show got repetitive after a while. That being said, each episode has a new intruiging story to get lost in. 

ADVENTURE, MYSTERY AND SCI-FI

OUTER BANKS

★★★★★

This show follows a group of teenagers as they navigate the dangers and secrets of the Outer Banks. The cinematography is stunning and the characters are loveable. The final season wrapped up the show nicely and was satisfying. This show is highly addictive and a great fast paced watch. 

STRANGER THINGS

★★★★★

Stranger Things is a science-fiction and mystery show with a really interesting storyline and a great group of characters. It has a mixture of suspense, adventure, friendship and supernatural elements, so there is always something happening. I would recommend this show if you enjoy shows that keep you guessing and have a lot of mystery and excitement. 

DARK MATTER

★★★★☆

Dark Matter is a science-fiction mystery show that can get quite confusing in the best way. It explores different versions of reality and has a lot of twists that make you want to keep watching to understand what is happening. I would recommend this show if you enjoy mystery, science fiction and shows that make you think. 

TEEN DRAMA

GOSSIP GIRL

★★★★★

Gossip Girl is a very dramatic show that follows a group of wealthy teenagers living in New York. There is a lot of romance, friendship drama, secrets and unexpected twists throughout the series. I really like how there is always something happening, which makes it very easy to binge. I would recommend this show if you enjoy drama and romance. 

THE O.C

★★★★★

The O.C. is a classic teen drama set in California that follows a group of teenagers and their families. The show has a really good mix of romance, friendship, family drama and comedy. It is also quite nostalgic because of when it was made. I would recommend this show if you enjoy older teen dramas with lots of character development. 

ONE TREE HILL

★★★★★

One Tree Hill is a longer teen drama that follows a group of friends as they grow up and deal with relationships, family, school and basketball. There are a lot of different storylines, so there is always something new happening. I would recommend this show if you enjoy emotional dramas with romance, friendship and lots of character development. 

GINNY AND GEORGIA

★★★☆☆

Ginny & Georgia is a drama about a mother and daughter who move to a new town and try to start a new life. The show has a really good combination of family drama, romance, friendship and comedy. I would recommend it if you enjoy shows with complicated family relationships and a mixture of funny and more serious storylines. 

HEARTBREAK HIGH

★★★☆☆

Heartbreak High is a modern high-school drama that follows a group of teenagers through friendships, relationships and everyday school life. I really like how the show feels quite realistic while still being funny and entertaining. It also covers a lot of different issues that teenagers can relate to. I would recommend it if you enjoy modern teen dramas. 

GLEE

★★☆☆☆

Glee is a high-school musical comedy-drama that combines singing and dancing with friendship, romance and plenty of drama. There are lots of musical performances throughout the show, which makes it really entertaining to watch. I would recommend this show if you enjoy music, comedy and high-school dramas. It is also a really easy show to binge. 

TEEN ROMANCE

MY LIFE WITH THE WALTER BOYS

★★★★☆

This show is about a young girl who moves across the country to live with a family that has 12 children. It is full of love laughter and loss and the plot lines include lots of depth and strong character development. This is a show you will find yourself extremely invested in. 

THE SUMMER I TURNED PRETTY

★★★★☆

The Summer I Turned Pretty is a show about a messy love triangle between a girl and two brothers. Lots of fans are dedicated to supporting one or the other and this show is seriously addicting and entertaining. You will find yourself extremely frustrated at times but it will be worth it! 

FINDING HER EDGE

★★★☆☆

Finding Her Edge is a teen drama that combines figure skating with romance. The first few episodes are hard to watch and some of the characters are intollerable, however, the plot has depth and lots of deep themes that make it intruiging. By the end of the season you will be hooked! 

XO KITTY

★★★☆☆

This show is a spin off of the To All The Boys I've Loved Before movies. It is a really good show to binge watch as the episodes are short and the seasons are quick. It is really easy to get invested in and find yourself rooting for the characters to end up together. Quite a few scenes are cringe and awkward but it's still a fun watch. 

SURVIVING SUMMER

★★☆☆☆

Surviving Summer is a teen drama set around surfing in Australia. It has a really fun beach setting and focuses on surfing, friendship, competition and romance. I really like how energetic the show feels, and it is an easy one to binge. I would recommend it if you enjoy sports, beach settings and teenage drama. 

GEEK GIRL

★★☆☆☆

Geek Girl is a fun and light-hearted teen show about a girl who unexpectedly gets involved in the modelling world. It is quite awkward and cringe but is a great representation of friendship, romance and the fashion industry without taking itself too seriously. I would recommend this show to anyone who has read the books and enjoyed them, becuase the creators were able to bring the characters to life really well.
